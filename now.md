# November 2026

I thought I'd update this page at least once a month.  I certainly had moments
where I thought of updating it.  But never did.

Any way…  I've finished _Island_ a log time ago and I didn't like it.  Here's my
review

> Reading this book was not enjoyable.  It starts off great but then it never
> kicks off.  Chapters go by and the main character continues grounded to a
> hospital bed.  Dialogues feel like monologues, given how long one goes until a
> reply.  The whole book feels like it's just one character explaining to
> another one about the ways and customs of Pala.  I would have enjoyed it if
> the main character would have find out about it by figuring it out on his own.
> I enjoyed the last paragraphs somewhat but there's some build-up that is
> missing.
